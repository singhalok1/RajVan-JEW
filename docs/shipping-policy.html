Shipping Policy

Clear delivery timelines and shipping support for your RajVan Jewelry orders.

Order Processing

Ready-to-ship products are usually processed within 24-48 hours after order confirmation. Custom name pendants are made specially for you and dispatch in 5-7 business days.

Delivery Timeline

Standard delivery usually takes 4-6 business days after dispatch. Express delivery, where available, usually takes 1-2 business days after dispatch.

Shipping Charges

Prepaid orders above Rs. 999 are eligible for free standard shipping. Other delivery charges are displayed during checkout.

Tracking

Once dispatched, tracking details can be shared through email, SMS, or WhatsApp when backend fulfillment is connected.
